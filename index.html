How To Play

  • WASD — move   Mouse — look
  • Space — jump (Creative: double-tap to toggle flight, then Space/Shift = up/down)
  • Left Click — break block   Right Click — place block / use
  • 1-9 or Scroll — select hotbar slot
  • E — open Creative block picker (Creative) or Crafting menu (Survival)
  • F — eat selected food (Survival)
  • Shift — sprint / descend while flying
  • Esc — release mouse / close menus
